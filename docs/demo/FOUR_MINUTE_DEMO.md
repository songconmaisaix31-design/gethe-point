# Four-Minute Fixture Demo

This script demonstrates one fictional household. It is not production authentication, live delivery, medical advice, or real-device Robot A3 evidence.

## 0:00–0:35 — Three roles and private input

1. Reset the Fixture.
2. Switch between the primary, partner, and subject projections.
3. On the subject projection, show “腿又疼了，下楼有点吃力” in the private chat.
4. Confirm that neither non-speaker projection contains that text.

## 0:35–1:05 — Explicit consent

1. Let the subject choose “share with the family space.”
2. Show the newly visible, speaker-authorized evidence in the primary projection.
3. State that a self-only choice creates no shared signal, task, domain, or report effect.

## 1:05–1:40 — Persisted responsibility

1. Open the neutral responsibility report.
2. Point out `discoveredBy`, `deadlineKeptBy`, `scheduledBy`, `executedBy`, and `followedUpBy`.
3. Read the neutral summary. Do not describe a person as lazy, irresponsible, ranked, scored, or diagnosed.

## 1:40–2:35 — Blocked and atomic handover

1. Show the health-domain handover as `blocked` because `last_report` is missing.
2. Add the missing report information; verify ownership has not moved.
3. Record the primary confirmation; verify ownership still has not moved.
4. Switch to the partner and record the second confirmation.
5. Show the single accepted snapshot: domain owner and every open future reminder move to the partner, while the primary burden count changes from 7 to 2.

## 2:35–3:35 — Deterministic care and truthful notifications

1. Let the subject explicitly activate the draft care rule.
2. Trigger the reminder and show App `shown_in_app` plus Robot A3 `disabled`.
3. Trigger the same logical event again and show `deduplicated`.
4. Advance the Fixture clock by exactly 60 seconds. Equality is timed out.
5. Show escalation order: partner, then primary. Acknowledge and close the event.

## 3:35–4:00 — Truth and reset

1. Show the Fixture truth label and safe notification log.
2. Reset the demo.
3. Verify the draft care rule, blocked handover, empty care events, and empty notification log are restored.

## Presenter Guardrails

- Say “shown in the App,” not “received,” unless the user acknowledgement exists.
- Say “Robot A3 is disabled in this Fixture,” not “the robot delivered it.”
- Say “integration acceptance requires the final Domain and Web branches,” not “full E2E passed on the QA branch.”
